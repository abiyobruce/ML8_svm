# Reports and design figures

- [`commit-file-allocation.md`](commit-file-allocation.md) lists every project file by member/module owner, plus shared files and directories.

## Weekly reports

- `week-01_Majorine.md`, `week-02_Majorine.md`, `week-03_Majorine.md`, and `week-04_Majorine.md` are compiled summaries: each gives the team-wide progress for that week.
- `week_01/`, `week_02/`, `week_03/`, and `week_04/` contain the per-member reports and explicitly labelled status records. Those files give each member's module-specific information; filenames include the member name.
- Some Faith and Jeremiah reports were recovered from the repository's historical Git commits and are identified as such in the file.
- A status record is used where no member report could be found. It describes repository evidence without claiming personal work or AI usage. Missing historical AI-use details are marked unrecorded in `ai-use-declaration.md`.
- `week-04_Majorine.md` is the compiled final project-status report. All ten Week 4 member files are final module-status reports based on repository evidence available on 24 October 2026. Member-specific Week 4 activity and AI-use details that were not recorded are not guessed.

## Final and AI-use reports

- `final-report.md` covers the project modules, design, measured program results, verification, and known limitations.
- `ai-use-declaration.md` summarizes the tools and purposes stated in member reports, the final integration assistance, and historical details that were not recorded.
